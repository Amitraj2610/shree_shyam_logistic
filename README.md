# 🚚 Shree Shyam Logistics – Official Business Website

> A professional website developed for Shree Shyam Logistics to showcase its transportation services, fleet capabilities, industry expertise, and business information.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://amitraj2610.github.io/shree_shyam_logistic/](https://amitraj2610.github.io/shree_shyam_logistic/)

## 📌 About the Project

Shree Shyam Logistics is a business website designed to establish a professional online presence for a logistics and transportation company based in Ankleshwar, Gujarat, India.

The website provides visitors with information about the company's transportation services, specialized vehicles, industries served, achievements, and contact details. It helps potential customers explore available logistics solutions and connect with the company for business enquiries and transportation requirements.

The primary objective of this project is to create an attractive, informative, and user-friendly digital platform that builds customer trust and improves the company's online visibility.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Professional Homepage:** Attractive landing page highlighting the company's logistics services and business identity.
- **About Us:** Information about the company, its experience, operations, and transportation capabilities.
- **Transportation Services:** Showcase of different vehicle categories and transportation solutions.
- **Fleet Showcase:** Dedicated presentation of specialized vehicles and equipment.
- **Industry Coverage:** Information about industries served, including:
  - Agrochemicals
  - Bulk Chemicals
  - Petrochemicals
  - Polymers
  - Paints & Adhesives
  - Pharmaceuticals
  - FMCG
  - City Gas Distribution
  - Glass Industry
- **Achievements & Recognition:** Highlights of company milestones, awards, and achievements.
- **Certifications:** Display of associated certification and compliance information.
- **Request for Quote:** Enquiry section for customers interested in transportation services.
- **Contact Information:** Business address, phone numbers, and email details.
- **Tracking Navigation:** Dedicated navigation option for shipment tracking.
